How to Play

The Objective

Someone has been murdered inside the asylum. Your job is to figure out what happened before the other patients do.

Search the asylum for clues and determine four things: the murderer, the victim, the method, and the room where the murder took place. Once you think you know the answer, find the Warden and make your accusation.

Starting the Game

At the beginning of each game, the asylum is generated and the details of the murder are chosen at random. Players begin at different locations, and turn order is randomized.

The Warden moves too. He takes his turn after the patients have taken theirs, so don't count on finding him where you left him.

Taking Your Turn

When your turn begins, roll to determine how far you can move. Travel through the halls, enter rooms, and investigate anything that looks suspicious.

Other patients can block your path in the hallways, so the shortest route isn't always available. Rooms and passages may provide other ways around the asylum.

Searching for Clues

Searchable locations are scattered throughout the asylum. Investigating one may uncover clues about the murder — or absolutely nothing useful.

Clues help eliminate possibilities from the four parts of the case: murderer, victim, method, and location. Keep searching and use what you've learned to narrow down the possibilities until only one solution remains.

Not every search is guaranteed to produce a clue. Apparently, an asylum is full of things that have nothing to do with this particular murder.

Your Journal

You don't need to memorize everything you discover. Your journal keeps track of the information you've gathered during the investigation.

Use it to eliminate possibilities, review what you've learned, and decide when you know enough to risk an accusation.

The Other Patients

Everyone is investigating the same murder, but only one player can solve it first. Other players may know things you don't, and communication can be useful — assuming you believe what they're telling you.

Public and private chat let you share information, misdirect your competition, cooperate temporarily, or simply accuse everyone else of being incompetent.

Making an Accusation

Knowing the answer isn't enough. You need to report it.

Find the Warden and get close enough to make your accusation. You'll need to identify the murderer, victim, method, and location correctly.

The Warden does not wait around for you. If he moves, you may have to chase him down before someone else gets there first.

Wrong Accusations

Accusing someone without enough evidence has consequences. A wrong accusation sends you back and costs you valuable time, giving everyone else a chance to finish the investigation while you recover.

Sometimes it's worth taking the risk. Sometimes you've just handed someone else the game.

Winning

Be the first player to find the Warden and correctly identify the murderer, victim, method, and location.

Search carefully. Pay attention to what you've eliminated. Decide who you trust. And when you're sure you know what happened, go find Napoleon.
