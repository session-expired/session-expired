# Planned assets

## This document acts as a roadmap to keep the art assets cohesive and planned

## Visual Art
* Character sprites will be drawn 64px 64px
* The basic room size is undetermined, may be tiles or large rooms with filler tiles
* There will be twelve rooms, on a 3x4 board
* Portraits will be at least 256px 256px, but may be more on the high-res side

## Audio assets
* A main loop for the lobby, game-play and win/loss screens, around one minute each
* Sounds that are injected at random times to raise the suspense
* Movement and interaction sounds
* Possibly message sent and message received sounds as well
